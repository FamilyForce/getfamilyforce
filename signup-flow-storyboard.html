Key Design Decisions
✂️
No Path C
Everyone starts with a 7-day trial. Want the full year? Just don't cancel. Simpler for users, same outcome.
📖
Library, not picker
Free users land on the real dashboard. They explore all 5 titles and pick freely, including Course and e-book options.
🐔
Escape hatch × 2
Child setup and payment both have a soft "browse free books" exit. Landing page keeps the Scout upsell alive for when they're ready.
🎟️
25% referral code
Applied on payment screen. Summary updates live. $79.99 → $59.99. "You save $20.00" shown clearly.

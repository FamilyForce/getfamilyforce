This month's priority

Introduce a bottleCloses in 3w

The window for introducing a bottle to a breastfed baby without a fight is roughly weeks 3 to 16. Most parents find out about this one too late -- week 4 is the sweet spot.

The move

Give one bottle of expressed breast milk per day. Let someone other than mom offer it if possible -- she can smell mom and will hold out.

Also worth knowing this month

Daily tummy time

Tummy time builds the neck, shoulder, and core strength Gabbie needs to roll, sit, and eventually crawl. Babies who skip it often show motor delays that take months to catch up.

The move

Aim for 3-5 sessions a day, even just 3-5 minutes each. Right after a diaper change works well.

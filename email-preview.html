Peanut introduction window

The window for introducing peanuts to reduce allergy risk closes around 7 months. Waiting longer significantly increases the likelihood of developing a peanut allergy.

What to do

Sitting with support milestone

By 7–8 months, most babies can sit with minimal support. This is a key motor milestone that your pediatrician will check at the 9-month visit.

What to do

Practice supported sitting during floor play — use pillows behind her, note how long she holds position unsupported

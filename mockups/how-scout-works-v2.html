Design notes — v2 changes vs. current

→ No card grid. 4 equal cards with emoji icons is the most generic "how it works" pattern on the web. Replaced with a left-rail vertical timeline — each step flows into the next, which is how the product actually works.
→ Product previews integrated into step 3. The email and calendar mockups were disconnected from the steps above them. Now they live inside step 3, where they belong. This is the product's core promise — it gets the visual weight it deserves.
→ Step 3 is the hero. Tinted background, larger layout, full-width treatment. At Airbnb, the payoff moment in a flow always got extra visual weight. The email + calendar IS the product. Treat it that way.
→ Outcome labels per step. "30 seconds to set up" / "196 windows, ranked for your child" / "Arrives on your child's monthly birthday" — these do more work than just "Add your child." They answer the implicit question: what do I get from this step?
→ Left-border colour coding on windows. Amber for closing, purple for open, grey for coming. Consistent with the dashboard. The visual language starts teaching the user how to read Scout before they've even signed up.
→ Step 2 shows actual data. Instead of "Scout maps your windows" with an abstract description, the user sees exactly what a window list looks like. The "+192 more tracked in background" line handles the "but why only 4?" objection.

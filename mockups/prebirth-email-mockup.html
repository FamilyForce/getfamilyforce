Your due date is April 28. Here's what to have ready before she arrives.

Before Olivia arrives

Safe sleep setup

A safe sleep environment reduces the risk of SIDS and accidental suffocation. The AAP recommends a firm, flat surface with no soft bedding, bumpers, or positioners.

The move

Set up the crib or bassinet before the birth: firm mattress, fitted sheet, nothing else inside. Practice this now so it's second nature at 3am.

Car seat installation

Rear-facing is the safest position for infants and toddlers. The seat needs to be installed and inspected before hospital discharge — you can't leave without it.

The move

Install the infant car seat rear-facing now and get it checked at a certified Child Passenger Safety inspection station before your due date.

Feeding plan

Whether breastfeeding or formula-feeding, having a plan before arrival reduces stress in the first 48 hours when decisions are hard and support is limited.

The move

If breastfeeding, schedule a consult with a lactation consultant before birth. If formula-feeding, have 1–2 ready-to-feed bottles on hand for the first day home.

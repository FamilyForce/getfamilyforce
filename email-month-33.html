Heads up: One window closes this month — meaning the natural developmental timing is ending. These are worth doing first.

This month's priority

Daytime dryness — what it means nowCloses in 0w

Most children achieve reliable daytime dryness between 24 and 36 months. If Leo isn't dry yet, month 33 is a natural checkpoint — and there are a few common reasons why it stalls.

The move

Stay consistent with the potty routine: on waking, before meals, before leaving the house, before nap, and before bed. Consistency drives dryness more than any other factor.

Also worth knowing this month

Balance on one foot — 2 seconds

Single-leg balance is a core measure of gross motor coordination at this age. It's also a prerequisite for hopping, skipping, and the kind of running that doesn't end in a fall.

The move

Turn it into a game: flamingo pose, stepping stones, counting how long he can hold it.

Getting dressed — simple clothes independently

Independent dressing is both a motor milestone and a self-efficacy milestone. Children who dress themselves arrive at preschool more confident.

The move

Lay out tomorrow's clothes the night before and step back in the morning. Remove time pressure and the process becomes much smoother.

Get ready for next month

36-month well-child visit

Prep: Book Leo's 3-year well-child visit now. Prepare a summary of what he can do: speech clarity, self-help skills, social interaction. This is the final Scout milestone checkpoint.

Telling stories — recall and narrative

Prep: After outings, ask open-ended questions: "What happened at the park today?" Give him time to organise the memory. Narrate back what you heard to model storytelling structure.

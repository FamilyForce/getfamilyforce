Hi Randy,

Welcome to Scout. You’ve got 29 days until your due date — which means there are a handful of things worth doing now, before you’re too tired to think straight.

I got a lot of this wrong with my first son because nobody told me what actually mattered in advance. Here’s the short list.

Do before April 16

Set up a safe sleep space

Babies need to sleep on a firm, flat surface — no pillows, bumpers, or soft toys. This is one thing worth getting exactly right before the baby arrives. It’s a 20-minute setup that can wait until you’re exhausted.

The move

Firm mattress, tight-fitted sheet, nothing else in the crib. No bumpers, no pillows, no stuffed animals.

Choose a pediatrician

Your baby’s first appointment happens within 48–72 hours of birth. If you haven’t chosen a pediatrician yet, you’ll be making that call from the hospital. Most good practices fill up fast.

The move

Call 2–3 pediatric practices this week. Ask if they’re accepting newborns and book a meet-the-doctor visit before April 16.

Pack your hospital bag 29 days

One in five babies arrives early. The bag that’s packed at 37 weeks is the one that actually makes it to the hospital. The one you’re going to pack “this weekend” is the one you forget.

The move

Pack before 36 weeks. Essentials: insurance card, birth plan, phone charger, going-home outfit for baby, loose clothes for you.

What happens when the baby arrives

1. Come back to Scout and confirm your baby arrived. Your prep windows close, and 196 developmental windows open.

2. Your first month is free — no card needed. Scout will send your first digest email automatically.

3. Every month after that, on your baby’s birthday, a new digest lands in your inbox.

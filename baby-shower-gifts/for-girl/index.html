Baby Shower Gifts for a Girl — What Actually Gets Used

Most "baby shower gifts for a girl" lists are just regular gift lists in pink. The gender doesn't change what a baby needs. Here's what actually matters — and what actually gets used past the first month.

The Pink Problem

Walk through any baby store and you'll find the girl aisle: pink onesies, floral swaddles, rose-gold accessories for a person who doesn't know what rose-gold is. Most of it gets outgrown in weeks. None of it addresses what new parents of a girl actually need in the first year.

A baby girl has the same developmental milestones as a baby boy. The same sleep regressions, the same language windows, the same screening appointments. The best gifts aren't gendered — they're useful. Here's what that looks like.

What to Skip for a Baby Girl Shower

  • Newborn-size clothes in pink. Outgrown in 2–3 weeks. Buy 3–6 month or 6–9 month size and they'll actually get worn.
  • Decorative nursery accessories. Gifts for the giver's eye, not for the parent's day. Skip the wall art and give something useful.
  • Pink versions of registry items. Check the registry. If they chose a specific color, don't add a second version in a "girl" shade.
  • Headbands and hair accessories for newborns. Adorable in photos. Uncomfortable for a baby who doesn't know what a headband is yet.

The question that fixes every gift decision

Frequently Asked Questions

What are good baby shower gifts for a girl?

The best gifts for a baby girl are practical, long-lasting, and useful well beyond the newborn stage: Scout by FamilyForce (monthly milestone emails from birth to age 3), a quality white noise machine, muslin swaddle blankets in 4-packs, a baby carrier, meal delivery credits, and diapers in sizes 1 and 2. Skip the pink-coded gear and focus on what actually helps.

Do baby shower gifts need to be pink for a girl?

No — and the most useful gifts aren't. A baby girl's developmental milestones, sleep patterns, and needs are identical to any other baby's. The color of the gift doesn't change how useful it is. Gifts like Scout, white noise machines, carriers, and meal delivery credits work for any baby.

What size clothes should I give as a baby shower gift for a girl?

Buy 3–6 month or 6–9 month size. Most babies outgrow newborn clothing in 2–3 weeks — and some skip it entirely. Note what season the size will hit so the parents know when to use it. Better yet, skip clothes entirely and give something that doesn't get outgrown.

What is a thoughtful baby shower gift for a new mom having a girl?

The Frida Mom postpartum recovery kit paired with a meal delivery credit is genuinely thoughtful — it's for the mom, not just the baby. Add a Scout subscription so the parents know what's coming every month for three years. Together, these three gifts cover the first weeks and the first three years. Nobody else at the shower will have thought of all three.

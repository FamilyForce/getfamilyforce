Amazon Gift Card

Instant delivery

The most flexible digital gift. Parents can use it for anything on the registry, a last-minute purchase after the baby arrives, or restocking essentials. Choose a meaningful amount ($50, $100, $150) and add a personal note. Send directly to their email from Amazon's gift card page.

Meal Delivery Gift Card — DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Instacart

Instant delivery

New parents don't have the energy to cook. A $50–$100 DoorDash or Uber Eats gift card means dinners taken care of for a week. An Instacart credit covers grocery delivery without leaving the house. These are among the most practically useful digital gifts you can give in the newborn phase.

Audible or Kindle Gift — Parenting Books

Instant delivery

An Audible credit or Kindle gift lets parents choose the parenting books that match their approach. Recommended books worth gifting: The Whole-Brain Child (Siegel & Bryson), Bringing Up Bébé (Pamela Druckerman), The Happiest Baby on the Block (Harvey Karp), or Brain Rules for Baby (John Medina). A credit covers one audiobook or ebook.

Tinybeans Premium — Photo Memory App Subscription

Digital subscription

Tinybeans is a private photo-sharing app for families — parents post photos and videos, grandparents and loved ones follow along. A Premium subscription (~$30/year) adds unlimited storage and privacy controls. It's one of the most-used apps among new parents in the first year and an ideal digital gift from a grandparent or family member who wants to stay connected.

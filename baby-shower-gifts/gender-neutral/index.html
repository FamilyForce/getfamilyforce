Gender-Neutral Baby Shower Gifts — The Best Options for Any Baby

Whether the gender is a surprise, not relevant, or you simply want to give something that works for any baby — these are the gifts that don't require knowing. They're also the most useful ones on this list.

Why the Best Baby Gifts Are All Gender-Neutral

Here's the thing about gender-neutral baby shower gifts: the most practical gifts have always been gender-neutral. A white noise machine doesn't come in pink. Meal delivery credits don't come in blue. Diapers don't need a gender. The things that actually help new parents in the first year are all things that work for any baby.

The "gender-neutral" framing is really just a way of saying: I'm giving you something useful, not something color-coded. That's a higher standard, and these gifts meet it.

White Noise Machine (Hatch Rest+ or Marpac Dohm) $50–$170

Used every single night from day one. Continuous white noise masks household noise during the light-sleep-phase wakings that become frequent after the 4-month sleep architecture shift. The Hatch Rest+ is white and minimal. The Marpac Dohm is the classic wooden-base version. Neither is gendered. Both are used nightly for years.

Muslin Swaddle Blankets — 4-Pack in Neutral Tones ~$40

Aden + Anais makes beautiful gender-neutral sets — sage, cream, warm grey, botanical prints. Four blankets because that's actually how many you need when you're going through them multiple times a day. The fabric is breathable muslin that gets softer with each wash. Stop swaddling once rolling signs appear (around 3–4 months) — that's a safety rule.

Baby Carrier (Solly Baby Wrap or Ergobaby Omni 360) $60–$200

Babywearing regulates a baby's nervous system through physical contact — fewer fussy periods, easier transitions. The Solly wrap is gentle and works beautifully from birth. The Ergobaby Omni 360 carries from newborn through toddler. Both come in gender-neutral colorways. Both are used daily for months.

Meal Delivery Credit — DoorDash, Uber Eats, Instacart $75–$100

The most gender-neutral gift of all: food. The postpartum period is exhausting and nobody wants to cook. A real credit — $75 or $100, not $20 — removes one genuine problem. Delivered digitally. Works for any family. Universally appreciated.

Newborn Photography Session Gift Card $150–$400

Newborn photography needs to happen in the first 5–14 days of life. Most parents intend to book it and run out of time. A gift certificate from a local newborn photographer removes that barrier. The photos from those 14 days don't exist again — and they work for any baby. Look for a photographer who specializes specifically in newborns, as the posing and safety skills are specialized.

Diapers + Wipes Bundle (Size 1 and Size 2) $50–$80

Not glamorous. Completely gender-neutral. Used multiple times daily, every day, for months. Buy Size 1 and Size 2 — not newborn, which most babies outgrow in 2–3 weeks. Add WaterWipes or Huggies Natural Care. This is the gift that gets used at 3am. That's the gift.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best gender-neutral baby shower gifts?

Scout by FamilyForce (monthly developmental milestone emails from birth to age 3), a white noise machine, muslin swaddle blankets in neutral tones, a baby carrier, meal delivery credits, diapers in sizes 1 and 2, and a newborn photography session gift card. All work for any baby, any family, any budget.

What to buy for a baby shower when you don't know the gender?

The easiest answer: give a digital gift that doesn't require any information about the baby. Scout by FamilyForce is delivered as a gift code and personalized when the parent redeems it — no gender, no name, no size needed upfront. Meal delivery credits and newborn photography gift cards work the same way. All three are genuinely useful and genuinely gender-neutral.

Are gender-neutral baby gifts less personal?

No — practical gifts are often more personal than gendered ones, because they signal that you thought about what the parents actually need, not what society expects you to give. A Scout subscription that helps a parent understand their baby's development every month is a far more personal gift than a yellow onesie you bought because you didn't know the gender.

What neutral colors work best for baby shower gifts?

White, cream, sage green, warm grey, and earthy terracotta all work well. The best gender-neutral gifts today have moved past the old "default to yellow" convention. Aden + Anais and BIBS both make beautiful neutral-toned products. For functional gifts like Scout, white noise machines, and meal delivery credits — color doesn't apply.

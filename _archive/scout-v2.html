Why it matters

Some windows
close forever.

The first three years of a child's life account for more developmental change than any other period. Language. Motor. Attachment. Cognition. Every domain is moving fast — and each has a window. A specific period when the brain is most receptive. Miss it and it closes.

Most parents learn about the peanut window after it's gone. They miss the speech check at 18 months. They start sleep training at 11 months instead of 6.

Not because they're bad parents. Because nobody told them when.

Scout tells you what to watch for, when to act, and what happens if you wait — before the window moves on without you.

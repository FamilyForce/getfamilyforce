## What FamilyForce Is

FamilyForce is a parenting education platform for parents of children aged 0–3 years.

Our core product is **Scout** — a monthly email digest delivered on the child's birthday each month. Scout tells parents exactly what developmental milestone windows are opening, peaking, and closing that month, what to do, what to watch for, and when to consult their pediatrician.

The content is used in two ways:
1. **Scout emails** — sent monthly to paying subscribers based on their child's exact age
2. **FamilyForce website** — published as milestone guides, blog posts, and educational articles

## What We Need From You

### Primary task: Clinical accuracy review
Please review each window against current guidelines from:
- American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
- Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)
- World Health Organization (WHO)
- Published peer-reviewed literature (where cited)

**Flag anything that is:**
- Factually incorrect
- Based on outdated guidance (superseded AAP/CDC recommendations)
- Potentially harmful if acted upon by a parent
- Missing an important clinical caveat or warning
- Overstated or understated in terms of urgency

**Note:** You do not need to rewrite the content — just flag issues with a comment and brief note on what should change. Our editorial team will handle rewrites.

### Secondary task: Timing accuracy
The open/peak/close week ranges are used to determine which parents receive which content. Please flag any windows where the timing range is clinically inconsistent with current developmental norms (e.g., if we say a milestone "closes at week 26" but the clinical standard is week 32).

### What you do not need to review
- Writing style, tone, or readability
- The email subject line templates (not included in this document)
- The marketing copy or product descriptions

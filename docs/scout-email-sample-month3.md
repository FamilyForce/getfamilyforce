**🟠 THREE THINGS BEFORE NEXT MONTH**

&nbsp;

**1. Tummy time — build to 20 minutes a day**

Not all at once. Two minutes after a diaper change. Three minutes on your chest while you watch TV. It adds up.

You're building the muscles he'll use to crawl and eventually walk. You're also preventing positional flat head — which is almost entirely preventable at this stage and significantly harder to address after month 6.

If he hates it: try it right after he wakes up, before he's fully realized what's happening.

&nbsp;

**2. Talk to him like he understands you**

He doesn't. And that's exactly why you should start now.

Narrating your day out loud — *"I'm putting on your sock, here comes the left foot"* — builds the neural wiring for language months before he says a single word. The AAP calls it serve and return. Every coo you respond to, every face you make back at him: that's a rep. The research on this is unambiguous. The window is open from birth. It's widest right now.

With my first son, I didn't know this. I thought talking to a baby who couldn't respond was a bit pointless. He turned out fine. His language development was slower than his brother's. The difference wasn't genetics.

&nbsp;

**3. ⚠️ If you're breastfeeding — introduce a bottle this month**

This one closes at 4 months and most parents miss it entirely.

The window for introducing a bottle to a breastfed baby without a fight is roughly weeks 3–16. After that, many babies flatly refuse anything that isn't the source. If you ever want a night out, a partner who can do a feed, or any flexibility at all — now is the time to try.

One bottle. A few times a week. That's the whole intervention.

## Design Notes

- **Subject line logic:** specific child name + specific number + specific intrigue. Preview text is the hook — "one window closes this month that most parents miss entirely" refers to the bottle intro.
- **3 things only above the fold.** Everything else is in the app.
- **Urgency tiering:** tummy time (important/ongoing), serve and return (important/ongoing), bottle intro (⚠️ closing — leads with the warning flag).
- **First Son moment:** woven into serve and return naturally — one paragraph, proves the point, doesn't detour.
- **Reassurance block:** one dedicated "don't worry about this" section. Gives permission to relax AND builds anticipation for next month.
- **Calendar invite:** positioned as a natural handoff at the end, not a technical footnote.
- **No exclamation marks.** No "you've got this." Jack's voice throughout.

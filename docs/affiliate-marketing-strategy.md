## BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT

Scout's affiliate program is open to everyone. No application. No gatekeeping. Every subscriber gets a unique link at signup. Every professional creator who finds the program gets the same link. One program. One commission rate. The distinction between "subscriber who tells a friend" and "postpartum doula with 15,000 followers" isn't about access — it's about volume, and volume determines how much they earn.

The commission is 25% for the lifetime of every subscriber they bring in. Not 12 months. Not until you decide to change it. For the life of the relationship. This is a credible, generous, sustainable signal that says: we value long-term partners, not one-time promoters.

**The infrastructure is already built.** Every signup already auto-generates a unique code stored in Supabase. Inbound capture is already live. The referral wireframe is already designed. This isn't a ground-up build — it's connecting dots that exist.

**What this program needs is execution, not more planning.**

## PART ONE: THE PROGRAM

### How It Works

Every Scout subscriber receives a unique referral code at signup — for example, `SARAH-A7B3` — derived from their name and a random suffix. This code is their affiliate link: `getfamilyforce.com?ref=SARAH-A7B3`.

When someone clicks that link and becomes a paying subscriber, Sarah earns 25% of every payment they make, for as long as they remain subscribed.

The referring subscriber gets 25% commission. The new subscriber gets 25% off their first payment. Two beneficiaries. One link. No friction.

### Commission Structure

| Plan | Price | Affiliate Earns | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $9.99/month | $2.50 | Every month subscriber is active |
| Annual | $49.99/year | $12.50 | Every year subscriber renews |
| 3-Year | $99.99 once | $25.00 | One-time at purchase |

**Lifetime means lifetime.** As long as that subscriber is paying, the affiliate is earning. This is the single most powerful signal you can send to a serious promoter. It says: bring us good customers and we will take care of you indefinitely.

### Commission Math — What Affiliates Actually Earn

Don't show affiliates percentages. Show them money. And don't lead with the per-payment number — lead with the cumulative earnings from a single piece of content over two to three years. That's the number that competes with what a sponsored post pays.

**For casual affiliates** (subscribers sharing with friends):

| Active referrals | Mix | Earnings |
|---|---|---|
| 5 monthly subscribers | Monthly | $12.50/month |
| 10 monthly subscribers | Monthly | $25/month |
| 25 monthly subscribers | Monthly | $62/month |
| 10 annual subscribers | Annual | $125/year |

**For serious affiliates** (KOLs, bloggers, educators with audiences) — lead with lifetime value from one piece of content:

| Referrals from one post | Over 1 year | Over 2 years | Over 3 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 annual subscribers | $250 | $500 | $750 |
| 50 annual subscribers | $625 | $1,250 | $1,875 |
| 100 annual subscribers | $1,250 | $2,500 | $3,750 |

A KOL who drives 50 annual subscribers from one genuine review post earns $1,875 over three years — from content they wrote once. That's the conversation that turns a curious creator into a committed partner. It also competes directly with what a one-time sponsored post pays, but keeps paying year after year.

### Cookie Duration

**90 days.** Parents research products for weeks. A first-time mom reads a blog post in week 32 of pregnancy and subscribes after the baby arrives. A 30-day cookie loses that conversion. 90 days is the right window for this audience.

### Payout

- Minimum balance: $25 before payout triggers
- Auto-payout on the 15th of each month
- Via PayPal or Wise (Rewardful mass payout)
- 30-day clearance window on new referrals (covers refund period)

The $25 minimum is important. It means dormant accounts with $0 never trigger payment processing. The vast majority of subscribers who never refer anyone cost exactly nothing to maintain as "affiliates."

## PART TWO: WHY THIS WORKS FOR SCOUT

### The Niche Advantage

Most startups launching an affiliate program have to go find their affiliates. Scout's affiliates already exist, already post daily, and are already trusted by exactly the audience Scout needs.

Postpartum doulas. Pediatric nurses. NICU nurses. Childbirth educators. Speech-language pathologists. Baby sleep consultants. Montessori educators. First-time mom bloggers. Parenting coaches. Postpartum fitness instructors.

Every one of these people has an audience of new and expecting parents. Every one of them is actively looking for quality products to recommend. Every one of them has more credibility with their audience than any ad Scout will ever run.

When a postpartum doula tells a new mom "I recommend Scout to every family I work with," that's not marketing. That's peer validation from the most trusted possible source. No amount of Facebook ad spend replicates it.

### The Product Advantage

Scout solves anxiety, not a want.

"Is my baby developing normally?" is a visceral, persistent fear that every parent of a child under three carries. An affiliate who can authentically say "this is what I use to stay ahead of my baby's milestones" is solving a real problem. Not recommending a gadget. Not pushing a discount code for something their audience doesn't need. Solving something their audience lies awake thinking about.

Products that solve real anxieties convert better, retain longer, and generate more authentic affiliate content than products that are simply nice to have.

### The Trial Advantage

Scout's free trial means affiliates can lead with zero friction: "Try it free." No credit card. No upfront commitment. The conversion from free to paid happens inside Scout's funnel after the subscriber has experienced the product. This is the same model that made Grammarly's affiliate program one of the highest-converting in the industry.

## PART THREE: WHAT WORKS — THE EVIDENCE

### ConvertKit: The Affiliate Army

Nathan Barry was at $2,000 MRR in 2013. Crowded market. No budget. His move: recruit every influential creator in his niche into a commission-earning partnership.

30% recurring commission. Personal outreach. Co-hosted educational webinars — not sales pitches, genuine value — with ConvertKit naturally embedded.

Results: Pat Flynn hosted one webinar → 1,000 signups in 24 hours → $5,000 new MRR in one month. In year one: 150+ webinars, 600%+ revenue growth. By 2019, affiliates drove $6M ARR — one-third of total revenue.

The lesson: the commission structure opened the door. The webinars drove the volume. The relationship sustained it.

### Grammarly: Freemium + Affiliate Flywheel

Free signup + premium upgrade model. $0.20 per free account + $20 per premium conversion. 90-day cookie. Top-performer cash bonuses. Detailed analytics so affiliates could see exactly what was working.

Conversion rates ran 20–30% — unusually high — because the product was free to try and genuinely solved a universal problem. Affiliates weren't asking their audience to take a risk. They were offering something useful for free.

The lesson: low-friction entry dramatically improves affiliate conversion rates. Scout's free trial is this lever. Use it in every piece of affiliate content.

### Notion: Community + Creator Flywheel

50% commission for 12 months. 13,000+ affiliates globally. The GTM insight: creators on YouTube and TikTok were already talking about Notion unprompted. Notion formalized those relationships, gave them a link, and funded authentic full-length usage videos.

The key finding: a 15-minute authentic YouTube video outperformed every paid ad they ran by orders of magnitude. Someone already invested 15 minutes in watching a creator use Notion is sold before the affiliate link appears.

The lesson: long-form authentic content from trusted creators beats paid ads at conversion. For Scout, this is a pediatric nurse doing a "what to expect in month 4" video with Scout embedded. That video lives on YouTube for years.

### What Consistently Failed

Across every program studied, the same failure patterns appeared:

**Setting it up and waiting.** Listing on a network and expecting affiliates to find you produces near-zero active affiliates. The program must be actively managed and actively promoted.

**No content assets.** Affiliates are busy. If promoting Scout requires writing from scratch, most won't. The program that makes it easiest to share wins.

**No activation sequence.** 50–70% of people who join an affiliate program never promote once. The difference between active and dormant affiliates is almost always what happened in the first 30 days. An onboarding sequence that gets someone to their first share in the first week changes everything.

**Wrong audience affiliates.** A 500,000-follower lifestyle influencer with a generic audience converts worse than a 5,000-follower postpartum doula with deeply engaged new-parent followers. Niche specificity is worth more than raw reach.

## PART FOUR: THE TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### What's Already Built

This is not a ground-up build. The following infrastructure already exists:

- **Code generation:** Every user who signs up gets `PREFIX-XXXX` auto-generated from their email prefix + random suffix. Stored in `profiles.referral_code` in Supabase and `localStorage.ff_referral_code`.
- **Inbound capture:** `?ref=` URL parameter already captured at sign-in, stored as `scout_referral_code` in localStorage, auto-applied at checkout.
- **Referral wireframe:** Complete dashboard UI with stats, code card, payout tracking, activity table, status badges — already designed and ready to port.
- **FRIEND-XXXX-XXXX discount codes:** Already applied as 25% off for new subscribers. This becomes the coupon attached to every affiliate link.
- **Stripe:** Live, connected, handling subscriptions. Rewardful integrates natively.

### What Needs to Be Built

1. **Rewardful account** — connect to Stripe, configure `?ref=` as the tracking parameter (not `?via=`), set commission to 25% lifetime, 90-day cookie. Half a day.
2. **Rewardful affiliate auto-creation** — on each new signup, call Rewardful API to create an affiliate account with the user's existing code as their slug. One edge function.
3. **`/earn` page** on getfamilyforce.com — the public-facing program page.
4. **Dashboard "Earn" section** — `/scout-dashboard/earn` — the in-dashboard affiliate management page (port from referral wireframe).
5. **Sidebar nav item** — add "Earn" to the Scout dashboard sidebar.
6. **Post-subscription modal** — fires once at trial-to-paid conversion.
7. **Dashboard home nudge card** — ambient, persistent, one line.
8. **Onboarding email sequence** — 5 emails over 30 days (drafted below).
9. **Footer link** — add "Earn" to site footer.

### Parameter Configuration

Rewardful supports custom tracking parameters. Configure it to use `?ref=` instead of the default `?via=`. This means the existing inbound capture logic in `sign-in.html` works without modification. No code change needed. Just a config toggle in Rewardful.

## PART NINE: RECRUITMENT STRATEGY

### The Math Behind the Targets

Realistic acquisition math so targets aren't arbitrary:

- Cold email outreach response rate for niche creator partnerships: ~15%
- Of those who respond positively, ~40% become active affiliates: ~6% of total outreach
- 10 personalised outreach emails/day × 20 working days = 200 emails/month
- 200 × 6% = ~12 new active affiliates/month from cold outreach
- Plus subscriber-driven signups (every existing subscriber is already in the program)
- Plus passive discovery via /earn page and footer

Conservative target: **15–20 new active affiliates per month** from Month 1. Subscriber-driven organic sharing on top.

KOLs with large, engaged audiences get a completely different approach. The standard invite — "here's a link, earn 25%" — undersells the opportunity and undervalues the relationship.

**Step 1: Send the product. Say nothing about the program.**

Identify the KOL's child's approximate age. Send them a personal note from Jack and a free annual Scout subscription. No ask. No pitch. Just:

> Hi [name],
>
> I follow your content and genuinely respect how you talk about [developmental milestones / evidence-based parenting / etc.]. I built Scout for exactly the parents you speak to.
>
> I'd love for you to experience it — no strings, no ask. I've set up a free annual subscription for you. Your first digest will arrive shortly, timed to [child's age].
>
> — Jack

That's it. Send the product. Let it do the work.

**Step 2: Follow up in two weeks.**

> Hi [name],
>
> Hope Scout has been landing well. Curious what you thought of the [month X] digest.
>
> If it resonated, I'd love to talk about what a partnership could look like. We have a straightforward earn program — 25% lifetime commission on every subscriber you bring in. For someone with your audience, that's real money from a single piece of content.
>
> One review post that drives 50 annual subscribers earns you $1,875 over three years. No sponsored post math required after that — it just keeps paying.
>
> Worth a quick conversation?
>
> — Jack

**Step 3: Lead with the 3-year content value, not the commission rate.**

When you get on the call, don't open with "25% commission." Open with: "One piece of content, written once, that earns you $X over three years." Then show the table. That's the conversation that closes a KOL partner.

## PART TEN: THE WEBINAR PLAY

This is the highest-upside tactic in the entire program. It should be the first thing executed after the program is live — not the third, not the fifth.

**The mechanic:** Find one affiliate with a genuine, engaged email list of new/expecting parents. Co-host a free 45-minute educational webinar. Topic: "Your Baby's First Year: What to Actually Expect." Jack presents the developmental milestones content (genuinely useful, evidence-based). Scout is demoed in the last 5 minutes, not pitched.

**Why it works:** The audience trusts the affiliate who invited them. They've committed 45 minutes, which means they're deeply engaged. By the time the Scout demo appears, they already want it because the preceding 40 minutes proved that the information Scout delivers is valuable.

ConvertKit: one webinar with Pat Flynn → 1,000 signups in 24 hours → $5,000 new MRR. Scout's numbers will be smaller. But a postpartum doula with a 10,000-person mailing list who invites 300 to a webinar and converts 30 is a genuine growth event.

**Offer for webinar registrants:** 60-day free trial instead of the standard 30 days. Exclusive to webinar attendees. This gives the affiliate a differentiated offer for their audience — something they can only get through this event.

**Target for first webinar:** 6 weeks after program goes live. Book it in Week 2.

## PART THIRTEEN: METRICS DASHBOARD

Track weekly. Review monthly. Adjust quarterly.

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| Links generated (total subscribers) | Program reach |
| Clicks on affiliate links | Affiliates who are actually sharing |
| Click-to-trial rate | How well your landing page converts |
| Trial-to-paid rate (affiliate traffic) | Product quality signal for referred traffic |
| Active affiliates (1+ click/month) | Program health |
| Commission payouts processed | Real money flowing, trust signal |
| Churn rate of affiliate-referred subscribers | Whether referred customers are good customers |
| % of new MRR from affiliate channel | Strategic impact |

The metric that matters most and is most often ignored: **churn rate of affiliate-referred subscribers.** If referred subscribers churn faster than organic subscribers, something is wrong — either the affiliates are reaching the wrong audience, or the trial experience isn't converting genuine interest into genuine product love. Track it from Month 1.

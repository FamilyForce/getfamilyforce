Nutrition 4 to 11 months
Peanut introduction
Window open
Introducing peanuts early reduces lifelong allergy risk by up to 80%. Miss this window and that protection is gone. Permanently.
SourceLEAP Study, Du Toit et al. · NEJM 2015 · NIAID/AAP Guidelines 2023
Screening 18 to 24 months
Autism screening window
Window open
Early identification changes outcomes. Intervention before age 3 is the most effective. You just need to know to ask for the M-CHAT screen.
SourceAAP Bright Futures Periodicity Schedule · M-CHAT, Robins et al. · CDC Early Intervention 2024

4-month sleep architecture

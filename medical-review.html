Why it matters

Parents deserve accurate information

Scout sends you developmental milestones timed to your baby's exact age. When you read that your 9-month-old should be doing something specific, that information needs to be right.

Babies develop on their own timelines — and the goal of Scout is never to cause anxiety, but to help you stay informed and catch things that genuinely matter. That only works if the underlying data is clinically sound.

That's why we had every developmental window in our database reviewed by a physician before we called it ready.

Our process

How the review works

  • 1
    Source-first research
    Every developmental window is grounded in established pediatric literature — AAP guidelines, CDC developmental milestones, and peer-reviewed research on child development.
  • 2
    Age window calibration
    Each milestone has an open, peak, and closing age window. These ranges are carefully calibrated to reflect normal variation in healthy development — not just average onset.
  • 3
    Physician review
    Dr. Sanchez reviewed all 214 windows for clinical accuracy — checking that age ranges, guidance, and framing reflect current pediatric standards and wouldn't cause undue parental anxiety.
  • 4
    Revision & sign-off
    Content flagged during review was revised and re-checked before being published. The review date is recorded with every update cycle.

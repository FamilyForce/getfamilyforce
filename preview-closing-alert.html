Introduce a bottle (breastfed babies) 7 days left

Around 6-8 weeks is the sweet spot. Before this, babies are still learning to breastfeed. After 3 months, many breastfed babies refuse a bottle entirely — and staying flexible about feeding makes a real difference when you're returning to work or need a night off.

The move

Have someone other than the primary breastfeeder offer the bottle. Use a slow-flow nipple. Timing matters: try when Emma is hungry but not frantic. One attempt a day, at the same time, is enough.

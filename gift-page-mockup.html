No. You just need an email address — yours to buy, theirs to receive it. No account, no app, no setup required on your end.
Can I give this before the baby is born? ›
Yes — and it's perfect for expecting parents. Scout has a pre-birth mode that sends relevant content during pregnancy, then automatically transitions to the baby's monthly digests from birth. The gift works from day one.
What if they already have a baby? ›
No problem. Scout picks up at the baby's current age — whether they're 2 weeks old or 18 months. The first digest arrives on the baby's next monthly birthday.

After purchase, you'll receive a printable gift card you can include in a physical card or gift bag. It looks beautiful printed.

In 5 years of child development research, we've never met a parent who didn't want to know what their baby needs. But if for any reason they're not happy, contact us and we'll sort it — no questions asked.

Every milestone window in Scout is drawn from peer-reviewed child development research. Not parenting forums. Not one person's opinion. Actual science, written to be useful for real parents.
